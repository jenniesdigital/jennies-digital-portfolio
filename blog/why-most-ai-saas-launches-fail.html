Why Most AI SaaS Launches Fail: The Zero-to-One GTM Playbook

Launching an AI product today is louder and more competitive than any previous software cycle. A great product launch is not an announcement—it is the orchestration of an inevitable movement.

The 5-Stage Zero-to-One GTM Framework

Stage 1: The Private Alpha Beta-Testing Core. Limit your first 50 users to high-intent power users who provide daily feedback on friction points.

Stage 2: The Provocative Category Manifesto. Write an essay challenging the status quo of your industry. Don't just launch a tool; launch a perspective.

Stage 3: The Interactive Sandbox Playground. Remove authentication friction. Allow visitors to experience the core value in the browser before asking for an email.

Stage 4: Co-Marketing With Design Partners. Launch alongside 3 recognizable enterprise logos whose quotes validate your ROI.

Stage 5: The Multi-Channel Distribution Blitz. Coordinate Product Hunt, LinkedIn executive thought leadership, niche newsletter sponsorships, and technical deep-dives on the same morning.
