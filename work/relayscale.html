PLG, DevTools · 2021–2023

Scaling Developer Growth to 350K+ Users

⚡ 350k+ Active Developers · 185% NRR · 3.2x Freemium Signups

1. The Strategic Challenge

RelayScale had immense engineering strength but suffered from high drop-off during onboarding and low conversion from free CLI users to paid enterprise clusters.

2. Go-To-Market & Positioning Strategy

Redesigned the developer onboarding journey with 1-click cloud sandbox deployments, contextual in-app upgrade triggers, and an automated Product Qualified Lead (PQL) scoring model that fed directly into the enterprise SDR team.

3. Quantified Business Impact

Scaled active developer community to 350,000+, increased self-serve trial conversion by 3.2x, and propelled NRR to an industry-leading 185%.
