Africa's Creator Monetisation Landscape

A competitive analysis of four platforms shaping how African creators make money online.

Deep-Dive Competitive Research Deck

Full teardown deck covering pricing, feature matrices, distribution moats, and strategic positioning of Selar, Mainstack, Nestuge, and Coachli.

VIEW THE PRESENTATION ▶

I analyzed four major creator monetisation platforms: Selar, Mainstack, Nestuge, and Coachli, to understand how they differentiate, who they serve, and where opportunities exist in an increasingly competitive market.

The question

How are African creator platforms positioning themselves in a rapidly growing but still underdeveloped monetisation market?

The competitive landscape

This is where I'd make your existing presentation do the heavy lifting.

Platform Positioning Best for Differentiator
Selar Commerce-first Digital product sellers Affiliate network + established trust
Mainstack Brand-led Brand-conscious creators Aesthetic storefront experience
Nestuge Automation/community Creators building systems Community + automated workflows
Coachli Service/expertise Coaches & experts Booking + live service delivery

Key insight

These platforms aren't really selling the same thing. They're competing around different ways of building an online business.

COMMERCE

Selar

Focuses on frictionless checkout, currency flexibility, and affiliate distribution for product creators.

BRAND

Mainstack

Focuses on sleek aesthetics, customizable link-in-bio storefronts, and premium visual identity.

AUTOMATION

Nestuge

Focuses on automated membership management, event gating, and community engagement infrastructure.

EXPERTISE

Coachli

Focuses on calendar integration, paid 1:1 sessions, webinars, and high-touch knowledge consultations.

What I learned

Features don't create differentiation on their own. Positioning does.

Looking across these four platforms showed me how products with increasingly similar feature sets can create very different market positions by choosing a specific customer, problem, and value proposition to own.

Looking beyond direct feature comparisons

It also reinforced the importance of looking beyond direct feature comparisons. Understanding why a product exists, who it is really for, and what alternative it is competing against can reveal opportunities that a simple competitor matrix misses.
